I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at the [University of Illinois Chicago](https://www.uic.edu/) since 2024, working with [Prof.Natalie Parde](https://www.natalieparde.com/). Prior to this, I earned my Bachelor’s degree from [Sichuan University](https://en.scu.edu.cn/). 

My research focuses on making large language model (LLM) training more efficient and better understood. I study how to make training more effective under limited computational budgets, particularly in supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). For example, I investigate how to identify the most informative training examples and reduce the computational cost of data selection.

I am also interested in developing better process and outcome reward models by leveraging **model-internal signals and representations** to evaluate reasoning quality and guide learning. Beyond improving training methods, I study how LLMs encode and acquire information internally, using techniques such as linear probing to understand how training shapes their representations and behavior. Ultimately, I aim to connect insights into how LLMs learn with practical methods for training more efficient, reliable, and interpretable models.
